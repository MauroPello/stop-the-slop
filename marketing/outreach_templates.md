## 1. YouTuber / Commentary Creator Outreach Email

Use this template when reaching out to YouTubers who cover internet culture, AI trends, YouTube commentary, or video essays (e.g. coffeezilla, moistcr1tikal, Ordinary Things, Wendover Productions, Colin & Samir, Neo, etc.).

**Subject Line Options**:
- `Quick open-source tool for spotting AI content farms on YouTube`
- `Built a free browser tool to detect AI slop on YouTube (open-source)`
- `For your coverage on AI content farms on YouTube`

**Email Body**:
```text
Hi [Creator Name / Team],

I've really enjoyed your coverage on [specific topic/video they made regarding AI slop, YouTube algorithms, or internet culture].

Over the past few months, I got increasingly frustrated watching search results and recommendations get inundated with automated "faceless" AI content channels (scraped ChatGPT scripts read by synthetic voices over random stock clips).

To help viewers spot these videos before clicking, I built and open-sourced a free browser extension called Stop the Slop (https://mauropello.github.io/stop-the-slop/).

How it works in 10 seconds:
1. It analyzes public caption transcripts in real-time.
2. It overlays an instant AI probability badge directly on YouTube thumbnails across Home, Search, and Recommendations.
3. Clicking the popup shows a sentence-by-sentence linguistic heatmap pinpointing exactly which lines triggered robotic AI markers.
4. It's 100% free, MIT-licensed, and collects zero user data.

Source Code: https://github.com/MauroPello/stop-the-slop
Firefox Store: https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/stop-the-slop-ai-video-detect/

I thought this might be useful for your research or interesting to your audience when discussing the rise of synthetic video churn. If you want to check it out or have any thoughts on improving detection accuracy, I'd love to hear your feedback!

Best regards,

Mauro Pellonara
Creator of Stop the Slop
support@mauropellonara.dev
https://github.com/MauroPello
```

**Subject Line Options**:
- `PITCH: Open-source browser extension detects the rise of "AI Slop" on YouTube`
- `Story Pitch: Fighting automated YouTube content farms with open-source edge AI detection`

**Email Body**:
```text
Hi [Journalist Name],

I saw your recent reporting on [generative AI, automated content farms, platform decay, or YouTube's algorithm].

As generative AI tools make it trivial to mass-produce synthetic videos at scale, YouTube feeds are experiencing an unprecedented surge in "AI Slop"—automated ChatGPT scripts voiced by cloned audio and uploaded in bulk for algorithmic ad capture.

To address this from the viewer's side, I've built and released Stop the Slop (https://mauropello.github.io/stop-the-slop/), a free, open-source browser extension for Chrome and Firefox.

Key Story Angles:
- The Counter-Slop Movement: Viewers taking platform curation into their own hands using client-side extensions (similar to SponsorBlock and Return YouTube Dislike).
- Explainable AI vs Black Boxes: Instead of an opaque score, the tool uses a sentence-by-sentence heatmap to expose common LLM transition formulas ("In today's fast-paced digital era...").
- Edge Architecture: Sub-50ms thumbnail badging powered by Cloudflare Workers + D1 database edge caching.
- Zero-Data Privacy: Built with strict zero-telemetry and open-source transparency (MIT License).

Code Repository: https://github.com/MauroPello/stop-the-slop
Interactive Demo: https://mauropello.github.io/stop-the-slop/
```
