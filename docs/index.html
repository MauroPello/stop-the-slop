Process

How detection works

Three straightforward steps to evaluate video scripts.

01

Read Transcript

When you open a YouTube video, the extension reads the available subtitle track directly on page.

02

Analyze Phrasing

The text is evaluated for typical LLM markers, stylistic uniformity, and repetitive sentence templates.

03

Display Score

View the overall probability score, verdict, and highlighted sentences in the popup or on thumbnails.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Stop the Slop free? +
Yes. Stop the Slop is free and open-source under the MIT license. There are no ads, paywalls, or subscriptions.
How does the detection score work? +
The extension checks video transcripts against linguistic markers typical of large language models. The score represents an estimated probability rather than an absolute proof.
What data does the extension collect? +
None. The extension does not collect personal data, search queries, or browsing history. It only accesses the video transcript on the YouTube watch page you inspect. See the Privacy Policy for details.
What if a video does not have captions? +
The extension relies on captions (creator-provided or auto-generated) to analyze the script. Videos with captions completely disabled cannot be analyzed.
